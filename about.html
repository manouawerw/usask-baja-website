Mission

Where we're going.

We're at the very beginning. Here is what we stand for and where we intend to take this team.

Mission statement

Our mission is to design, build, and race a competitive off-road vehicle, and through that work give University of Saskatchewan students the hands-on engineering, manufacturing, and leadership experience a classroom alone cannot.

Our vision

To build a lasting, respected Baja SAE program that brings the University of Saskatchewan to the podium and develops engineers ready for industry.

What we value

Our principles

01

Craftsmanship

We take pride in work that is designed carefully and built well.

02

Learning

No experience required. We teach, document, and pass knowledge forward.

03

Teamwork

Engineering, business, and design students working as one crew.

04

Saskatchewan pride

Representing USask and our province on a national stage.

The road ahead

Our roadmap

The path from a new team to a competitive program.

  1. Phase 01

    Foundation

    Recruit founding members, organize sub-teams, and secure our first partners and a place to build.

  2. Phase 02

    The first car

    Design, analyze, and manufacture our first vehicle, then test it on real terrain.

  3. Phase 03

    First competition

    Bring USask to a Baja SAE event, pass technical inspection, and finish the endurance race.

  4. Phase 04

    Contender

    Refine each design year over year and compete for top results.

  5. Phase 05

    Legacy

    A self-sustaining program with a strong alumni and partner network.
