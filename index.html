University of Saskatchewan · Baja SAE

Engineered on the prairie. Proven in the dirt.

USask Baja SAE is a new student team designing, building, and racing a single-seat off-road vehicle in the Baja SAE collegiate design series.

Join the team Our mission
Our mission

Our mission is to design, build, and race a competitive off-road vehicle, and through that work give University of Saskatchewan students the hands-on engineering, manufacturing, and leadership experience a classroom alone cannot.

Where we're going
The competition

What is Baja SAE?

Baja SAE is an intercollegiate competition run by SAE International. Student teams design and build a small off-road vehicle and then put it to the test, both in front of industry judges and on punishing terrain.

01

Design

Students engineer the frame, suspension, drivetrain, and controls from the ground up.

02

Build

Every tube is cut, welded, and machined by the team itself.

03

Compete

Design reviews, a business presentation, and dynamic events ending in a four-hour endurance race.
